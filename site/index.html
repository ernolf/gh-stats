Released since takes a date, or a period back from the last sample:

  • 2026-01-15 or 15.01.2026
  • 30 days or 30d
  • 2 weeks or 2w
  • 6 months or 6m
  • 1 year or 1y

Any number goes, in upper or lower case, with or without a space before the unit, and the unit in the singular as well. The slider sets the usual periods; an empty field, or the slider at ∞, sets no limit.
